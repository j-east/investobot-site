Investobot

Software to manage risk in leveraged index tracking ETFs

It's difficult to beat the S&P 500. So if you can't beat them, join them.

Leverage makes sense when the market is up and can be disastrous in a downturn. Investobot holds the S&P 500 through leveraged index funds, adds exposure while the market is above trend, and cuts it when trend or volatility breaks.

More of the market when it's working. Less when it isn't.

Deterministic rules, easy to follow. Your account, on your machine.

The rule

  • Step out when the S&P closes below its 150-day average, or 20-day volatility rises above 25%.
  • While out, hold half S&P and half T-bills.
  • Step back in after 3 closes in a row above the 100-day exponential average.

Trades happen at the close after the signal, about 5 times a year. You can read every line of the code.

How it runs and what you pay

  • Runs on a VPS you rent, connected to your brokerage account with your own keys.
  • You pick the setting and can switch it off at any time.
  • We keep no access after setup.
  • [Annual fee] a year, flat, for software support and running your VPS. You also pay your broker, and the funds charge about 0.9% a year.

What can go wrong

  • Day-to-day swings run larger than the S&P on every setting. Aggressive will probably lose more than half at some point.
  • Crashes can move faster than the rule. One bad day can cost a third or more at 3x.
  • The edge depends on market trends lasting. In simulated markets with the trends removed, switching rules lost to holding the S&P.
  • With T-bills at 6%, typical Aggressive falls to 13.5% and its worst 5% to 0.9%.
  • The future can be worse than anything in the record.

Taxes

Switching realizes gains, so the account type matters. Typical Aggressive return after federal tax:

  • IRA or Roth: 18.2% a year
  • Taxable, S&P held plus index futures: 16.7%
  • Taxable, leveraged funds: 15.3%

Assumes 24% ordinary and 15% long-term rates, no state tax. Talk to a tax professional.
